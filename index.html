Trading Simulator Mobile Application

Introduction

Welcome to Trading Simulator. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our mobile application, how that information is used, and how it is handled.

By using Trading Simulator, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Trading Simulator does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, phone number, or email address.

However, the application may collect or access the Google Advertising ID provided by your Android device. This identifier may be used for analytics, advertising-related measurement, attribution, and improving the application and its services.

  • No registration or login is required.
  • We do not directly collect your name, phone number, password, or postal address.
  • The Google Advertising ID may be collected or accessed for analytics and advertising-related purposes.
  • We do not intentionally collect precise location data through the application.
  • We do not intentionally collect the contents of your personal communications.

Google Advertising ID

Trading Simulator may use the Google Advertising ID, a resettable identifier associated with your Android device, for analytics and advertising-related purposes.

The Google Advertising ID may help us understand application usage, measure advertising performance, analyze installation and engagement trends, and improve the application's functionality.

The availability and behavior of the Google Advertising ID are controlled by your device and Android settings. Depending on your Android version and device manufacturer, you may be able to reset or limit the use of your advertising identifier through your device's privacy or advertising settings.

Analytics

We may use analytics services and technologies to understand how users interact with Trading Simulator, identify technical issues, measure application performance, and improve the user experience.

Analytics information may include application usage events, device or application information, and the Google Advertising ID or other identifiers made available by the relevant analytics service.

We use analytics information for legitimate business and product-improvement purposes and do not use it to directly identify you by name.

Local Storage

Your in-app simulation data, including your paper trading portfolio, watchlist, and simulation settings, is primarily stored locally on your device.

This locally stored application data is not intended to be transmitted to our servers. You can remove locally stored application data by clearing the application's data through your device settings or by uninstalling the application.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

Trading Simulator may use third-party services, including Google services, for advertising, analytics, measurement, attribution, and application improvement.

These third-party service providers may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, application usage information, and other information required to provide their services.

Third-party services operate according to their own privacy policies and terms. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of the third-party services used by the application for additional information about how they process data.

Children's Privacy

Trading Simulator does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13.

Because the application may use advertising and analytics services, information such as advertising identifiers may be processed by third-party service providers in accordance with their applicable policies and legal requirements.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application and to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, our services, applicable laws, or data practices.
