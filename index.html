Effective Date: June 25, 2026 Version 1.0

Introduction

Welcome to Trading Simulator. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information for our mobile application. By using Trading Simulator, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

No Data Collection

We believe in absolute privacy. Trading Simulator does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data or user information. Your activity remains entirely private to you.

  • No registration or login is required.
  • No personal identifiers (such as name, email, IP address, or device ID) are collected.
  • No location data is accessed or processed.
  • No usage analytics or tracking SDKs are integrated.

Local Storage

All application data, including your paper trading portfolio, watchlist, and simulation settings, is stored locally on your device. We use secure on-device database storage solutions. This information is never transmitted to our servers or any third parties. You can clear this data at any time by clearing the application cache/data or uninstalling the application from your device.

Third-Party Services

Trading Simulator operates as a standalone application and does not share data with third-party service providers. There are no advertising networks, external tracking tools, or social media integrations embedded in the application that could monitor your activity.

Children's Privacy

Since we do not collect any personal data, our application is fully compliant with children's privacy regulations. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly within the application or updated on our policy page. Your continued use of the application following the posting of changes constitutes acceptance of those changes.
